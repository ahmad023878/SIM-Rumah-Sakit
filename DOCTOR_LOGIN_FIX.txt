FINAL5 - Doctor login handoff fix

The doctor login now redirects to dashboard.php with a short-lived signed doctor_auth ticket.
The dashboard verifies the ticket against the doctors table and restores the session. This avoids
relying solely on CI4/legacy session handoff when legacy PHP files are requested directly.

After successful verification, the existing signed cookie/session are also restored.

Target URL after login:
http://localhost:8080/hospital-sim/hms/doctor/dashboard.php?doctor_auth=...
